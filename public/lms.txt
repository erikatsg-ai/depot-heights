# Depot Heights

> A neighbourhood guide to Depot Heights and the surrounding Bukit Merah area of Singapore, covering local amenities, food, transport, education, healthcare, recreation, shopping, and history.

Base URL: https://depot-heights.vercel.app/

## About

Depot Heights is an HDB residential estate on Depot Road in Bukit Merah, Singapore. This site helps residents and visitors explore the estate and nearby neighbourhoods, facilities, places to eat, green spaces, and transport connections.

## Main pages

- [Home](https://depot-heights.vercel.app/): Overview of the neighbourhood and links to the site's guides.
- [About Depot Heights](https://depot-heights.vercel.app/about-depot-heights/): Estate overview, including Blocks 102–114, Depot Heights Shopping Centre, and the surrounding Bukit Merah area.
- [Location](https://depot-heights.vercel.app/location/): Guide to Depot Heights' location and nearby neighbourhoods and destinations.
- [History of Depot Road](https://depot-heights.vercel.app/history-of-depot-road/): Background on Depot Road and the area's history.
- [Living Around Depot Heights](https://depot-heights.vercel.app/living-around-depot-heights/): Overview of neighbourhood lifestyle, everyday convenience, connectivity, and nearby destinations.
